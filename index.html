What it does

Modicus is a minimalist Android launcher. Swipe between screens:

  • favs: the apps you use most, as a list, as a cloud where the apps you use more appear larger and bolder, or as simple monochrome icons. It can also learn from what you open.
  • all: every app, a to z, with a letter bar to jump to a letter.
  • settings: how it looks and works.
  • widgets: a screen for your widgets. Premium, one-time purchase.

Black and white plus one gray, following your system theme, light or dark. Almost no animation. A quick menu at the bottom holds the apps you open all the time.

Optional notification dots show which apps have a notification waiting. If monochrome icons are hard to recognize, switch to full color in settings.

Privacy

Modicus has no internet permission. It collects nothing and sends nothing. Your favorites, settings and usage counts stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
