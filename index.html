What it does

Modicus is an Android launcher. It lists your installed apps alphabetically as plain text. Tap a name to open the app. There are no icons, no badges and no animations.

It follows your system theme, light or dark.

Privacy

Modicus has no internet permission. It collects nothing and sends nothing. Read the privacy policy.
