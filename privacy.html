Privacy policy for Modicus

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Modicus is a launcher app for Android, published by PBLIZ! B.V., Molenlaan 11 E, 1422 XN Uithoorn, the Netherlands.

Data collection

Modicus does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Installed apps and settings

To show your apps, Modicus reads the list of launchable apps on your device. To arrange your favorites, it also keeps a count of how often you open each app through Modicus, together with your favorites and settings.

All of this stays on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Removing the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Premium purchase

Modicus offers an optional one-time Premium purchase that unlocks a widgets screen. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Modicus does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium has been purchased on your account.

Widgets

Widgets are provided by other apps on your device. Modicus only displays them. Any data a widget handles is governed by the privacy policy of the app that provides it.

Children

Modicus does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
