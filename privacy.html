Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Modicus (modicus) is a launcher app for Android, published by PBLIZ! B.V., Molenlaan 11 E, 1422 XN Uithoorn, the Netherlands.

Data collection

Modicus does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Installed apps and settings

To show your apps, Modicus reads the list of launchable apps on your device. It also keeps your favorites, your settings and a count of how often you open each app through Modicus. Older use slowly counts less, so the favorites can follow your habits.

All of this stays on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Removing the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Notification dots

Notification dots are optional and only work if you switch on notification access in Android. Modicus then shows a small dot for apps that have a notification waiting.

For this, Modicus checks only which app a notification comes from, and whether that app is allowed to show dots in your Android settings. It never reads the title, the text or any other content of a notification. Ongoing notifications, such as music or navigation, are not counted.

The list of apps is kept in memory only. It is not saved, logged or sent anywhere, and it is emptied when you switch notification access off. You can switch it off at any time in Android's settings.

Premium purchase

Modicus offers an optional one-time Premium purchase that unlocks a widgets screen. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, through Google's Play Billing Library. Modicus does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium has been purchased on your account, and remembers the result on your device so it also works offline.

Widgets

Widgets are provided by other apps on your device. Modicus only displays them. Any data a widget handles is governed by the privacy policy of the app that provides it.

Children

Modicus does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
