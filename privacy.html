modicus

privacy policy

applies to modicus for android (com.modicus.app) · last updated 20 october 2026

in short

modicus collects nothing. it has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. everything it keeps stays on your phone.

no internet access

android's internet permission is removed from the app. modicus contains no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account and no third-party code that could send data.

what stays on your phone

  • your favorites and settings
  • how often you open apps, used only to sort favs when "learn by itself" is on. older use slowly counts less.
  • whether you bought premium

this data is stored only on your device. it is removed when you uninstall modicus or clear its data.

permissions

  • notification access (optional, you switch it on): used only to show a dot next to apps that have a notification waiting. modicus looks at which app has a notification, never at what it says, and keeps nothing.
  • list of apps: modicus only looks for apps that can be opened from a home screen, so it can show their names.
  • request uninstall: used only when you tap "uninstall" on an app. android always asks you to confirm.

purchases

premium is bought through google play. that purchase is handled by google, under google's privacy policy. modicus only learns whether premium was bought, and remembers that on your phone.

children

modicus collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

changes

if this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
