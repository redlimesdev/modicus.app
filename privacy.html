Privacy policy for Modicus

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Modicus is a launcher app for Android, published by PBLIZ! B.V., Molenlaan 11 E, 1422 XN Uithoorn, the Netherlands.

Data collection

Modicus does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Installed apps

To show your apps, Modicus reads the list of launchable apps on your device. This list is used only on your device to display and open apps. It is never sent anywhere and never stored outside the app.

Children

Modicus does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
